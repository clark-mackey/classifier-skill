# Jev use cases for a digital marketing agency (2026-10-04)

Created by [Clark Mackey](https://cakewebsites.com).

582 ideas drawn from a marketing agency's doctrine, standard operating procedures and service lines. Handling: automate 281, spot-check 234, human-review 65, do-not-build 2.
Sections: `doctrine` (marketing strategy and diagnosis advice), `sops` (step-by-step delivery procedures), `services` (each service split into delivery, client-product and agency-ops lenses, plus new-offer ideas). Untested ideas, not evidence.
